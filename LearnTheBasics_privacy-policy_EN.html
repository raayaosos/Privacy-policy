Privacy Policy

Application: تعلم الأساس (learn the basics)

Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Introduction

"Learn the basics" is an educational application designed to operate offline and respect user privacy.

2. Information Collection

The application does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It does not use advertising trackers or analytics services.

3. Internet Access

The application does not require or use internet connectivity to provide its functionality.

4. Location Information

The application does not access or collect device location information.

5. Local Storage

The application saves necessary files locally on the user's device. These files are not transmitted to the developer or external servers.

6. Third-Party Services

The application does not integrate third-party advertising or analytics services.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Files saved by the application remain on the user's device. Users can delete saved files using the available application or device storage controls. Uninstalling the application may also remove application data, depending on the device.

8. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Policy

Any changes to this Privacy Policy will be published on this page.
